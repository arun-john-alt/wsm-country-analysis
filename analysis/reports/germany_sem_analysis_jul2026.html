Issues, Opportunities & Recommendations

❌ Issues

  • Lead volume in free-fall: Google NB 354 → 149 (H1 pace). Spend cuts are directly cutting conversions in a market that converts well.
  • ADAP CPL inflation: $1,584 → $2,233 → $1,713. Peaked in 2025, improving. Review bid strategy to sustain H12026 gains.
  • ADSSP NB eliminated: 28 → 5 leads. Brand ADSSP (10 leads) now bigger than NB.
  • LOG360 search paused: 71 clicks in 2025, $25K spend. Effectively stopped.
  • SEO declining: 653 → 379 → 116 leads. 68 → 28 → 8 conversions. High quality but losing reach.

✅ Opportunities

  • Reallocate LOG360 & ADSSP NB to ELA Bing: LOG360 has zero conversions across 3 years ($29K H12026 spend wasted). ADSSP NB converts at only 3.6%. Shift these budgets to Bing ELA ($89/lead, proven channel) for immediate efficiency gain.
  • Shift ADAP Google NB budget toward Bing ADAP: Google ADAP CPL ($1,713) is 71% higher than Bing ADAP ($1,002). Partial reallocation from Google to Bing ADAP maintains conversion output at lower cost.
  • Review ADAP Google bid strategy: CPL peaked at $2,233 in 2025 and is recovering ($1,713 in H12026). Sustain this improvement — tighter bid controls could free up budget within the existing envelope.
  • Bing ADSSP: 8 leads H12026 — conversion follow-up needed in Q3: Growing channel, but no conversions yet. Prioritise sales follow-through before deciding further allocation.
  • Direct channel at 106 leads H12026 — highest stability: 6.6% conv rate maintained. No action needed; monitor for any drop.
