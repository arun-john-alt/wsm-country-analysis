Issues, Opportunities & Recommendations

❌ Issues

  • ADAP NB: $2,878 CPL, 0 conversions since 2024. Leads declining (52→29→10). Worst cost-efficiency of any channel or market.
  • Bing NB conv rate: 0% in H12026. Was 13.3% in 2024. 17 leads, 0 conversions. Investigate landing pages and follow-up.
  • ADSSP and ELA NB stopped: Both at near-zero from 2025 onwards. No plan visible.
  • SEO conv rate: 0.6% in H12026 (was 8.8% in 2025). Local /fr/ pages at 0 conversions. Leads still arriving but not closing.
  • Google NB conv rate improving (7.3% H12026) but lead volume so low (41 in H1) that absolute conversions remain limited.

✅ Opportunities

  • Reallocate ADAP NB to ADMP NB or Bing: ADAP NB CPL = $2,878 with 0 convs since 2024. ADMP NB at $1,384 CPL is still converting (3.5% in H12026). Alternatively, Bing NB CPL ($815) is ~53% cheaper than Google NB — shifting some ADAP NB budget to Bing ADAP/ADMP could recover volume at lower cost.
  • Fix ADAP Google NB bid strategy first: CPL has gone from $1,658 (2024) to $2,878 (H12026) — a 74% increase. Review bid caps, match types and negative keywords before any reallocation decision.
  • SEO & Sales joint investigation: Leads still arriving (161 in H12026) but 0.6% conv rate. Unlike a pure rankings issue, this may be a sales pipeline or reseller gap. Needs to be investigated with the Sales team.
  • Bing Brand showing promise: 30% conv rate in H12026 (3 convs from 10 leads). Very low CPL ($204). Protect this allocation.
