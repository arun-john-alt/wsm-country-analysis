Issues, Opportunities & Recommendations

❌ Issues

  • Conversion rate collapse (Google NB): 2.8% → 0.4% in 2025 despite stable spend. Sales/follow-up breakdown, not a traffic issue.
  • ADAP CPC tripled: ₹269 (Jan-24) → ₹821 (Jul-26). Same budget, 3× fewer clicks. Bid strategy review needed urgently.
  • ADAP CTR halved: 9% → 4% on Google. Ad relevance / landing page degradation for Brazil market.
  • ADSSP & ELA spend cuts: Spend being reduced without solving conversion problem first — risks further lead decline.
  • SEO conversion collapse: 26 → 14 → 1 conversions. Brazil organic visibility deteriorating across all major products — needs separate SEO & Sales investigation.

✅ Opportunities

  • Reallocate ADSSP NB to Bing ADAP: ADSSP Google NB is at 3 leads H12026 with $957 CPL and zero conversions. This budget is better deployed on Bing ADAP (which isn't running). Same envelope, better expected output.
  • Reallocate LOG360 Google NB to Bing ADMP/ADSSP: LOG360 search activity is near-zero with no conversions in 3 years. Bing ADMP ($272 CPL) and Bing ADSSP (10 leads H12026, growing) are better candidates for this allocation.
  • Fix ADAP bidding before further cuts: CPC tripled (₹269 → ₹821) and CTR halved (9% → 4%). A bid strategy review (target CPA or manual CPC) could recover 30–40% more clicks at the same spend — no new budget needed.
  • ADAP ad/LP refresh for Brazil: CTR issue points to creative or landing page mismatch for the Brazil market. Fixing relevance recovers clicks within existing spend.
  • Prioritise Sales investigation on conversions: Google NB conv rate collapsed from 3.1% to 0.4%. Until this is resolved, any additional SEM spend is inefficient. Fix the pipeline first, then reallocate.
  • SEO local /br/ pages: Zero conversions in H12026 from local pages despite 118 leads. Investigate with content/SEO team — no ad spend involved.
