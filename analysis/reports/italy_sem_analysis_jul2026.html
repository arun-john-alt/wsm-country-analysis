⚠️ Classification gap — "Cloud Branding" theme is Brand, not Non-Brand "Cloud Branding" is a branding theme for LOG360 Cloud but is not captured by the standard brand filter (Theme IN ('Branding','Log360 - Branding')). As a result, its leads and conversions are counted under Google NB in these reports. Impact: 7 of the 15 reported 2025 NB convs and 3 of 4 reported H12026 NB convs originate from Cloud Branding. Excluding it, true Google NB convs were 8 (2025) and 1 (H12026), with conv rates of ~3.5% and ~0.7% respectively — even worse than the headline 2.9%. Fix: add 'Cloud Branding' to the brand theme filter in themes_firstlast_semroi queries.

Issues, Opportunities & Recommendations

❌ Issues

  • Google NB conv rate: 2.9% in H12026 (true NB just 0.7% if Cloud Branding mis-tags are excluded). $115k spent for 4 reported conversions, only 1 of which is a true NB conv ($115k/conv). Conv rate fell from 6.5% (2025). Root cause unknown — bid strategy changes, match type drift, landing page degradation, or sales follow-up quality. Needs immediate investigation.
  • ADMP Google NB CPL at $1,197: Almost doubled from $623 in 2024. Zero tracked conversions in H12026 from ADMP NB despite being the 2nd highest-volume product (22 leads). Review bidding and keyword intent coverage.
  • "Cloud Branding" theme mis-tagged as Non-Brand: 7 of 2025 NB convs and 3 of H12026 NB convs originate from Cloud Branding (LOG360 Cloud brand theme). Fix the brand filter to include 'Cloud Branding' in themes_firstlast_semroi queries. True Google NB H12026 = 1 conversion.
  • Google Brand conv rate declining: 10.8% → 7.0% → 4.3%. Volume growing (brand investment paying off) but leads not closing at the same rate. Investigate sales pipeline for brand leads specifically.
  • Bing Brand 2025: 30 leads, 0 conversions. 13× scale-up in spend (2024 $425 → 2025 $5,754) resulted in 0 conversions. Recovered partially in H12026 (2 convs) but 2025 spend efficiency was very poor.
  • SEO lead volume declining sharply: 547 → 351 → ~202/yr pace (−42% vs 2025). Ranking losses on key /it/ and global pages reducing inbound pipeline.
  • ADMP Bing CPL: $1,521 in H12026 (4.7× from 2024 $325). Zero conversions ever from ADMP Bing. Review or pause.

✅ Opportunities

  • Reallocate from Google NB to Bing NB: 10.3% conv rate in H12026, $5,959/conv vs Google NB's $28,778/conv — Bing NB is 4.8× more efficient per conversion. Redirect budget from Google NB underperformers (ADMP at $1,197 CPL / 0 convs) to Bing NB, particularly ELA/LOG360 (18 leads at $326 CPL) and ADAP Bing (6 leads at $714 CPL).
  • Investigate Google NB conv rate collapse: Before reallocating further, understand why conv rate fell (2025: 6.5% → H12026: 2.9%, true NB: 0.7%). Check: landing page A/B tests, bid strategy changes, new campaign types, or sales follow-up quality for Italy SEM leads.
  • Maintain Google Brand allocation: $393 CPL with 4.3% conv rate — still far more efficient than NB. Volume growing (53 leads in H12026 vs 52 in all of 2024). Maintain current investment.
  • SEO recovery plan: Leads declining sharply (−42% YoY) but conv rate holding at 8.9%. An SEO audit to recover lost rankings on /it/ product pages would multiply conversions without additional ad spend.
  • Fix Cloud Branding classification: Add 'Cloud Branding' to the brand theme filter. This will correctly move LOG360 Cloud brand convs out of NB reporting and give a true picture of Google NB performance.
  • Bing Brand at $176 CPL: Very efficient. 2 convs in H12026 from 16 leads. Small but positive signal — maintain allocation.
